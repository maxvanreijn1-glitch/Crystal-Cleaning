About Crystal Cleaning

A family run cleaning business without the hasstle.

Crystal Cleaning is run as a direct, family service. No third party's

The approach

You'll always know exactly who's cleaning your home

Most cleaning companies work through a rota — whoever's available gets sent to your address that week. It's efficient for the company, but it means you never quite know who will be coming into your house, and if a job's rushed or something's missed, you end up explaining it to a call centre rather than the person who did the work.

Crystal Cleaning doesn't work that way. There's no dispatcher between you and the clean. We quote the job ourselves, We turn up to do it, and if there is any trouble you will deal with us directly.

A simpler way to keep your property clean

This allows us to give our customers a personal service handled with care. Right now that's regular cleaning, deep cleaning, end of tenancy and move-out cleaning — a short, honest list rather than a long menu.
