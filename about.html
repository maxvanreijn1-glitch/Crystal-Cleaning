About Crystal Cleaning

Small on purpose

I'm Ellie — I started Crystal Cleaning in August 2026 after six years cleaning other people's houses for other people's companies. Here's the actual reason why, not the polished version.

Founder
How it started

I got tired of apologising for other people's mistakes

Before Crystal Cleaning, I worked for two cleaning agencies in Newcastle — first as a cleaner, then running a small team. Both jobs taught me a lot about the actual work. Neither taught me anything good about how the industry treats customers, or cleaners, for that matter.

The pattern was always the same. A client would sign up expecting consistency, and instead get whoever was free that week. Cleaners were shuffled between postcodes with barely any notice, paid by the job rather than the hour, so nobody was rewarded for taking their time. And when something went wrong — a missed spot, a late arrival, a broken vase — the client wasn't talking to the person who'd been in their house. They were talking to a booking line reading from a script, three steps removed from what actually happened.

I kept thinking: this isn't hard to fix. It just needs someone to actually be there.

So in August 2026 I registered Crystal Cleaning, bought a better hoover than the one the agencies gave us, and started taking on clients directly. It's me, plus two cleaners I trust completely — Priya and Tom, both DBS-checked, both people I'd happily hand my own house key to.

If you want a national chain with an app and a loyalty scheme, that's not what this is. If you want to know exactly who's coming into your house, and have their actual number if a Tuesday needs to move to a Thursday, that's what you get here.

Ellie DoddsFounder, Crystal Cleaning
How I run things

A few things I'm not willing to compromise on

Same cleaner, every visit

You shouldn't have to re-explain your home, your alarm code, or where the recycling goes every time someone new turns up. Once you're matched with a cleaner, that's who comes — holiday cover aside, and I'll always tell you in advance if it's someone different.

Paid by the hour, not the job

Every cleaner on the team is paid properly for their time, not rushed through a job to hit a quota. It's the reason people actually stick around, and the reason the work doesn't get sloppy in month three.

One price, agreed upfront

No "from £X" pricing that turns into something else once someone's stood in your hallway. I quote based on what you tell me about your home, and if anything changes on the day, we talk about it before, not after.

You can always reach me

Not a form, not a chatbot — my actual mobile number. If something's not right, I want to know that day, not find out through a bad review three weeks later.

The team, in full

All three of us

This is genuinely the whole company right now. We're growing carefully — I'd rather turn down a booking than send someone I haven't trained myself.
